## 🧠 What I Do

I enjoy working where **data, technology, and real-world decision-making** meet.

- 📊 **Business Intelligence:** Building dashboards, performance reports, and semantic models that turn complex metrics into clear stories.
- ⚙️ **Data Engineering:** Creating reliable data pipelines, validating and reconciling data, and making reporting reproducible.
- 📈 **Applied Economics:** Using forecasting, impact evaluation, and scenario analysis to understand what drives outcomes.
- 🤖 **AI & Agentic Workflows:** Exploring how language models and intelligent agents can make analytical work faster, smarter, and more efficient.
- 🌾 **Agri-Food Analytics:** Digging into profitability, agricultural cooperatives, and value-chain performance to support better economic decisions.

**My philosophy?** Good analytics shouldn't just explain what happened. It should help answer *"So, what do we do next?"* 💡
